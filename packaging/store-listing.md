## Short description

Convert files by pressing Shift while you drag them. No app to open, no upload.

## Description

Start dragging files in File Explorer, press Shift, and a dial of formats opens under your cursor. Drop on a
format and the converted file appears next to the original.

Press Ctrl+Shift instead for tools: trim a video, crop or resize a photo, compress a PDF, merge PDFs,
extract an archive, even one with a password.

Everything runs on your PC. There is no account, no upload and no telemetry, and your originals are never
overwritten.

Converts images (HEIC, AVIF, WEBP, PSD, camera RAW and more), video, audio, PDF, Word, Excel and
PowerPoint, e-books, archives, data files, subtitles, fonts and 3D models.

## Features

- Shift mid-drag opens a dial of formats that fit the files
- Ctrl+Shift opens tools: trim, crop, resize, compress, merge, extract
- The dial turns into a progress gauge while it works
- Batch conversion of many files at once
- Works offline; no account and no telemetry
- Never overwrites a file; a cancelled job leaves nothing behind
- Light and dark themes
