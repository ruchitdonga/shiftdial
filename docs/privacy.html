Privacy

Shiftdial, last updated 19 October 2026.

What Shiftdial collects

Nothing. Shiftdial has no account, no telemetry, no analytics and no ads, and it makes no network connections. Files you convert are read and written only on your own PC.

What it reads while running

  • Whether the left mouse button, Shift and Ctrl are held, so it can open the dial during a drag. It does not record keystrokes.
  • The files you drop on the dial, to convert them. Results are saved next to the originals.

What it stores

Your settings and a log of conversion errors, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Shiftdial on your PC. They never leave it. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version deletes them too.
